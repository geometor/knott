=======
Persons
=======

Mathematicians, geometers, and scholars referenced across the **GEOMETOR Knott Mathematical Catalog**.

.. _person-knott:

Dr. Ron Knott (fl. 1979–2025)
-----------------------------

Mathematician & computer scientist (formerly Dept. of Mathematics, University of Surrey); author and curator of Fibonacci Numbers, the Golden Section and the Golden String (1996–2025).

.. _person-euclid:

Euclid of Alexandria (fl. c. 300 BCE)
-------------------------------------

Author of the Elements; formalized division in extreme and mean ratio (II.11, VI.30), the golden 72°–72°–36° triangle and regular pentagon/15-gon (IV.10–16), and the icosahedron/dodecahedron (Book XIII).

.. _person-fibonacci:

Leonardo Pisano (Fibonacci) (c. 1170 – c. 1240–1250)
----------------------------------------------------

Pisan mathematician whose Liber Abaci (1202) introduced Hindu-Arabic numerals to Europe and posed the monthly rabbit problem generating 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, …

.. _person-pacioli:

Luca Pacioli (c. 1447 – 1517)
-----------------------------

Franciscan friar and mathematician; authored De Divina Proportione (1509, illustrated by Leonardo da Vinci) cataloging thirteen geometric effects of the Golden Ratio across plane and solid figures.

.. _person-kepler:

Johannes Kepler (1571 – 1630)
-----------------------------

Astronomer and geometer; discovered the (1, √Φ, Φ) right triangle (1597 letter to Mästlin), observed F_{n+1}/F_n → Φ (1608), and studied the rhombic triacontahedron.

.. _person-ptolemy:

Claudius Ptolemy (c. 100 – c. 170 CE)
-------------------------------------

Alexandrian astronomer-mathematician whose Almagest chord theorem (Book I.10) yields the pentagonal diagonal-to-side identity d = Φ s and exact trigonometric tables.

.. _person-pythagoras:

Pythagoras of Samos (c. 570 – c. 495 BCE)
-----------------------------------------

Founder of the Pythagorean school; associated with the pentagram badge (ὑγίεια) and right-triangle quadrance additivity.

.. _person-cassini:

Giovanni Domenico Cassini (1625 – 1712)
---------------------------------------

Italian-French astronomer and mathematician who presented F_{n-1} F_{n+1} - F_n^2 = (-1)^n to the Royal Academy of Sciences in 1680.

.. _person-euler:

Leonhard Euler (1707 – 1783)
----------------------------

Swiss mathematician whose Machin-type rational arctangent decompositions underpin the reciprocal Fibonacci telescoping series for π/4.

.. _person-gauss:

Carl Friedrich Gauss (1777 – 1855)
----------------------------------

Proved at age 18 (30 March 1796; Disquisitiones Arithmeticae, 1801) that the regular 17-gon is constructible by straightedge and compass via nested quadratic radicals.

.. _person-binet:

Jacques Philippe Marie Binet (1786 – 1856)
------------------------------------------

French mathematician who published (1843) the closed-form power formula F_n = (Φ^n - (-φ)^n)/√5 previously known to Euler, Daniel Bernoulli, and de Moivre.

.. _person-catalan:

Eugène Charles Catalan (1814 – 1894)
------------------------------------

Belgian-French mathematician who generalized Cassini's identity to F_n^2 - F_{n-r} F_{n+r} = (-1)^{n-r} F_r^2 (1879).

.. _person-lucas:

Édouard Lucas (1842 – 1891)
---------------------------

French number theorist who named the Fibonacci sequence, introduced the companion Lucas sequence L_n = Φ^n + (-φ)^n, and developed Fibonomial coefficients.

.. _person-loyd:

Sam Loyd & Sam Loyd Jr. (1841 – 1911 / 1873 – 1934)
---------------------------------------------------

American puzzle pioneers who popularized both the 64 = 65 vanishing-square dissection and the 64 = 63 notched-rectangle dissection.

.. _person-dudeney:

Henry Ernest Dudeney (1857 – 1930)
----------------------------------

English mathematical puzzle creator who analyzed generalized Fibonacci chessboard dissections for both +1 gap and -1 overlap paradoxes.

.. _person-hurwitz:

Adolf Hurwitz (1859 – 1919)
---------------------------

German mathematician who proved (1891) that :math:`|\alpha - p/q| < 1/(\sqrt{5}\, q^2)` has infinitely many rational solutions for every irrational α, with √5 optimal specifically because of Φ.

.. _person-thompson:

D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson (1860 – 1948)
---------------------------------------

Scottish biologist, mathematician, and classicist whose On Growth and Form (1917) analyzed Fibonacci phyllotaxis and gnomonic biological growth.

.. _person-wythoff:

Willem Abraham Wythoff (1865 – 1939)
------------------------------------

Dutch mathematician who characterized cold positions of Wythoff's Nim (1907) as the complementary Beatty pairs (⌊nΦ⌋, ⌊nΦ^2⌋).

.. _person-zeckendorf:

Édouard Zeckendorf (1901 – 1983)
--------------------------------

Belgian physician, army officer, and amateur mathematician who published the canonical non-consecutive Fibonacci representation theorem (1972, discovered c. 1939).

.. _person-lehmer:

Derrick Henry Lehmer (1905 – 1991)
----------------------------------

American number theorist who posed (1936) the reciprocal Fibonacci arctangent series π/4 = ∑_{k=1}^∞ arctan(1/F_{2k+1}).

.. _person-coxeter:

Harold Scott MacDonald Coxeter (1907 – 2003)
--------------------------------------------

Geometer whose Regular Polytopes and Introduction to Geometry unified 3D golden polyhedra, 5-cube compounds, and cylindrical phyllotaxis lattices.

.. _person-penrose:

Roger Penrose (b. 1931)
-----------------------

Mathematical physicist and geometer who discovered the five-fold aperiodic P2 (kite & dart) and P3 (thick & thin golden rhomb) tilings in 1973–1974.

.. _person-conway:

John Horton Conway (1937 – 2020)
--------------------------------

Mathematician who named Penrose's 'kite' and 'dart' prototiles and connected Fibonacci shift operations with Wythoff's array.

.. _person-stolarsky:

Kenneth B. Stolarsky (b. 1942)
------------------------------

Number theorist who constructed the Stolarsky interspersion array (1977) via nearest-integer Golden Ratio rounding.

.. _person-kimberling:

Clark Kimberling (b. 1942)
--------------------------

Mathematician who formalized interspersion and dispersion arrays generalizing the Wythoff and Stolarsky arrays, and curated the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC).

.. _person-bergman:

George M. Bergman (b. 1943)
---------------------------

Mathematician who discovered and published the base-Φ ('phigit') positional numeral system in 1957 at age 14.

.. _person-wildberger:

Norman J. Wildberger (b. 1955)
------------------------------

Creator of Rational Trigonometry (Divine Proportions, 2005), replacing transcendental distance and angle with exact algebraic quadrance and spread.
