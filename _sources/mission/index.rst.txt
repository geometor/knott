===================================================
Mission — Mathematical Cataloging & Source Respect
===================================================

.. enunciation::

   A **GEOMETOR Mathematical Catalog** indexes the mathematical facts,
   constructions, sequences, and theorems curated in a copyrighted modern
   reference without reproducing its copyrighted prose or illustrations—pairing
   direct attribution and section-level deep links with 100% original symbolic
   proofs and synthesized ``.euclid-graphic`` diagrams.

Why a Mathematical Catalog?
---------------------------

Since **1996**, :person:`Dr. Ron Knott <knott>` (formerly of the Department of
Mathematics at the University of Surrey) has authored and continuously expanded
*Fibonacci Numbers, the Golden Section and the Golden String*
(:cite:`KnottFibHub`), one of the richest and clearest mathematical web
compendia ever created. Across dozens of interconnected pages, Dr. Knott gathers
Euclidean and modern compass-and-straightedge constructions of :math:`\Phi`,
exact radical trigonometric tables, three-dimensional polyhedral coordinates,
:person:`George Bergman <bergman>`'s irrational base-:math:`\Phi` ("phigit")
arithmetic, the recursive Rabbit / Golden String sequence, :person:`Édouard
Zeckendorf <zeckendorf>`'s Fibonacci base, :person:`Willem Wythoff <wythoff>`'s
and :person:`Kenneth Stolarsky <stolarsky>`'s interspersion arrays, Fibonacci
Pythagorean triples, :person:`Giovanni Domenico Cassini <cassini>`'s area
jigsaw paradoxes, and reciprocal Fibonacci arctangent series for :math:`\pi`.

In the **GEOMETOR** initiative, historical public-domain treatises—such as
:person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s *Elements* (:cite:`EuclidElements`), :person:`Luca
Pacioli <pacioli>`'s *De Divina Proportione* (:cite:`Pacioli1509`), and
:person:`D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson <thompson>`'s *On Growth and Form*
(:cite:`Thompson1917`)—are published as full Digital Scriptorium *Editions*
derived from archival folios. For living, copyrighted modern works (**©
1996–2025 Dr. Ron Knott, University of Surrey**), full-text reproduction is
neither appropriate nor desired. Instead, GEOMETOR constructs a **Mathematical
Catalog**:

1. **Zero Copyrighted Text or Media Reproduction**: Not a single prose sentence,
   HTML markup block, or GIF/PNG diagram is copied from
   ``r-knott.surrey.ac.uk``. Mathematical theorems, sequences, and geometric
   relations are universal facts; Dr. Knott's pedagogical writing and figures
   belong exclusively to him, and readers are encouraged to visit his cited
   pages directly.
2. **Exact Section-Level Attribution**: Every catalog entry (``KNOTT-1.1``
   through ``KNOTT-6.5``) records the exact page title, full clickable URL +
   ``#anchor``, and copyright credit to Dr. Ron Knott, University of Surrey.
3. **100% Exact Symbolic Verification**: Every cataloged construction and
   identity is modeled in ``geometor.knott`` over :math:`\mathbb{Q}`,
   :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})`, :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\Phi})`, or explicit
   cyclotomic radical towers with **zero** ``float`` or ``sp.Float``
   approximations, verified via Rational Trigonometry invariants (:term:`quadrance`,
   :term:`spread`, :term:`cross-ratio`) and polynomial reductions.
4. **Original Interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVGs**: Every entry renders a
   clean, theme-adaptive vector diagram synthesized directly from its
   ``geometor.model.Model`` instance and wired to inline ``:g:`` and ``:eq:``
   prose highlights.
5. **Cross-Corpus GEOMETOR Concordance**: Every entry connects Dr. Knott's
   hub to ``geometor/euclid``, ``geometor/elements``, ``geometor/pacioli``,
   ``geometor/wildberger``, ``geometor/divine``, ``geometor/etc``,
   ``geometor/phyllotaxis``, and ``geometor/trees``.
