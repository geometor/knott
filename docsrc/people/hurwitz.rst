Adolf Hurwitz
=============

German mathematician who proved Hurwitz's theorem (1891) on Diophantine approximation by rational numbers, establishing that √5 is the optimal irrationality constant specifically because of Φ.

Explicitly, the inequality :math:`|\alpha - p/q| < 1/(\sqrt{5}\, q^2)` has infinitely many rational solutions :math:`p/q` for every irrational number :math:`\alpha`, and the constant :math:`\sqrt{5}` cannot be increased without excluding the golden ratio :math:`\Phi`.
