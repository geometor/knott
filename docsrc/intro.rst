**GEOMETOR Knott** (``geometor-knott``) is an exact symbolic **Mathematical
Catalog** of the planar and solid Golden Ratio constructions, constructible
pentagonal and :math:`3^\circ` trigonometry, base-:math:`\Phi` ("phigit")
numeral systems, Sturmian Golden String morphisms, Zeckendorf representations,
Wythoff and Stolarsky interspersion arrays, and Fibonacci–Lucas–:math:`\pi`
identities curated by :person:`Dr. Ron Knott <knott>` in his landmark online
reference:

* **Curated Source**: *Fibonacci Numbers, the Golden Section and the Golden
  String* — `https://r-knott.surrey.ac.uk/Fibonacci/fib.html <https://r-knott.surrey.ac.uk/Fibonacci/fib.html>`_
  (**© 1996–2025 Dr. Ron Knott, University of Surrey**).

Unlike GEOMETOR's public-domain Digital Scriptorium *Editions* (such as
``geometor/euclid`` or ``geometor/pacioli``), **GEOMETOR builds Mathematical
Catalogs for copyrighted modern works**: zero prose paragraphs or graphic
files are copied from Dr. Knott's site. Instead, each of the **22 catalog
entries** (``KNOTT-1.1`` – ``KNOTT-6.5``) across **6 thematic collections**
formulates an independent GEOMETOR mathematical enunciation, cites Dr. Knott's
exact URL and ``#anchor`` so readers explore his rich exposition directly,
proves every geometric and algebraic invariant from scratch in 100% exact
``SymPy`` and ``geometor.model`` (`0` ``sp.Float`` atoms), renders an original
interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVG, and links every result across the wider
GEOMETOR ecosystem.
