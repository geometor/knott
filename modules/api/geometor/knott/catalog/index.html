geometor.knott.catalog¶

Master registry of the 6 Collections and 22 Catalog Items (KNOTT-1.1 – KNOTT-6.5).

Maps every cataloged theorem, construction, sequence, and identity to Dr. Ron Knott’s exact University of Surrey source URL and section anchor, its exact SymPy / geometor.model validator function, scholarly apparatus keys, and cross-referenced GEOMETOR repositories.
