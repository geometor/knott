geometor.knott.solid_geometry¶

Collection 3: 3D Golden Geometry of Solids (KNOTT-3.1 through KNOTT-3.3).

Catalogs the exact 3D algebraic coordinates, edge lengths, dihedral spreads, volumes, 5-cube compounds, and acute/obtuse golden quasicrystal rhombohedra curated by Dr. Ron Knott on phi3DGeom.html, paired with exact rational axonometric 2D projections into geometor.model.Model.

geometor.knott.solid_geometry.project_3d_to_2d(pt3: Point3D) → tuple[sympy.Expr, sympy.Expr]¶

Project an exact 3D point (x, y, z) to 2D via an injective rational cabinet matrix.

Uses exact rational coefficients \((X, Y) = (x - \frac{2}{5} y,\; z - \frac{1}{4} y + \frac{1}{7} x)\) so no floating-point approximations enter geometor.model.Model and symmetric polyhedral vertices remain distinct in the plane.

Parameters:

pt3 – Exact 3D coordinate triple (x, y, z).

Returns:

Exact 2D coordinate pair (X, Y).

geometor.knott.solid_geometry.dodecahedron_vertices_3d() → dict[str, Point3D]¶

Return the 20 exact vertices of the regular dodecahedron of edge \(2\phi\).

Combines the 8 cube vertices \((\pm 1, \pm 1, \pm 1)\) (C1..C8) with the 12 cyclic permutations of \((0, \pm\phi, \pm\Phi)\) (D1..D12).

Returns:

Ordered mapping of 20 vertex IDs to exact 3D coordinate triples.

geometor.knott.solid_geometry.construct_orthogonal_golden_rectangles_solids() → geometor.knott.flat_geometry.GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-3.1: Three orthogonal golden rectangles & regular icosahedron/dodecahedron.

Verifies in 3D that:

  • The 12 corners of three mutually perpendicular \(2 \times 2\Phi\) golden rectangles form a regular icosahedron with 30 edges of length 2, 20 equilateral faces, exact volume \(20\Phi^2 / 3\), and rational dihedral spread \(4/9\);

  • The 20 vertices \((\pm 1, \pm 1, \pm 1) \cup \operatorname{cyc}(0, \pm\phi, \pm\Phi)\) form a regular dodecahedron with 30 edges of length \(2\phi\), exact volume \(8 + 4\Phi\), and rational dihedral spread \(4/5\).

Returns:

Verified GeometricCatalogResult for KNOTT-3.1.

geometor.knott.solid_geometry.find_five_cubes_in_dodecahedron() → list[tuple[str, ...]]¶

Discover all 5 inscribed cubes of edge 2 among the 20 dodecahedron vertices.

Each cube is an 8-element subset of dodecahedron_vertices_3d() in which every vertex has 3 orthogonal neighbors at exact 3D quadrance 4 (face diagonal of a regular pentagon of side \(2\phi\), since \(\Phi \cdot 2\phi = 2\)).

Returns:

Sorted list of 5 8-tuples of vertex IDs partitioning the 60 face diagonals.

geometor.knott.solid_geometry.construct_cube_in_dodecahedron_model() → geometor.knott.flat_geometry.GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-3.2: Inscribed cube & compound of five cubes in a dodecahedron.

Constructs the 20-vertex regular dodecahedron of edge \(2\phi\), highlights primary inscribed cube C1..C8 of edge 2 (whose 12 edges are one face diagonal on each of the 12 pentagonal faces), and verifies all 5 inscribed cubes partition the 60 face diagonals with every dodecahedron vertex shared by 2 cubes.

Returns:

Verified GeometricCatalogResult for KNOTT-3.2.

geometor.knott.solid_geometry.construct_golden_rhombohedra_model() → geometor.knott.flat_geometry.GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-3.3: Acute & obtuse golden rhombohedra (3D quasicrystal tiles).

Spans the acute (prolate) golden rhombohedron from icosahedral vectors \(u_1=(0,1,\Phi), u_2=(1,\Phi,0), u_3=(\Phi,0,1)\) and the obtuse (oblate) golden rhombohedron from \(v_1=(0,1,\Phi), v_2=(1,-\Phi,0), v_3=(-\Phi,0,-1)\) (translated alongside for visual comparison). Every rhombic face has exact diagonals \(2\Phi\) and 2 (ratio \(\Phi : 1\)), exact volumes \(V_{\text{acute}} = 2\Phi^2\) and \(V_{\text{obtuse}} = 2\Phi\) (ratio \(\Phi : 1\)), and 10 of each assemble the rhombic triacontahedron of volume \(20\Phi^3\).

Returns:

Verified GeometricCatalogResult for KNOTT-3.3.
