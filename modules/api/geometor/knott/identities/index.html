geometor.knott.identities¶

Collection 6: Fibonacci-Lucas Identities, Pythagorean Triples, Jigsaw Paradoxes & \(\pi\) (KNOTT-6.1 – KNOTT-6.5).

Catalogs the Fibonacci generator of Pythagorean right triangles, Cassini’s identity and both \(64 \leftrightarrow 65\) (\(+1\) gap) and \(64 \leftrightarrow 63\) (\(-1\) overlap) jigsaw dissections, Binet/Lucas/Catalan/d’Ocagne/Honsberger and Jarden-Fibonomial power identities, Lehmer/Hayashi Fibonacci arctangent telescoping series for \(\pi\), and the Golden Angle parastichy lattice curated by Dr. Ron Knott on pythag.html, fibpuzzles2.html, fibFormulae.html, fibpi.html, and fibnat.html.

geometor.knott.identities.fibonacci_pythagorean_triple(n: int) → FibonacciPythagoreanRecord¶

Generate and verify the Fibonacci Pythagorean triple for index \(n \ge 1\).

Uses Euclid’s generator \((m, k) = (F_{n+2}, F_{n+1})\):

  • \(a_n = m^2 - k^2 = (F_{n+2} - F_{n+1})(F_{n+2} + F_{n+1}) = F_n F_{n+3}\);

  • \(b_n = 2 m k = 2 F_{n+1} F_{n+2}\);

  • \(c_n = m^2 + k^2 = F_{n+1}^2 + F_{n+2}^2 = F_{2n+3}\).

Parameters:

n – Positive integer (\(n \ge 1\)).

Returns:

Verified FibonacciPythagoreanRecord.

geometor.knott.identities.construct_fibonacci_jigsaw_model() → geometor.knott.flat_geometry.GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-6.2: Cassini’s \(8 \times 8 = 64 \leftrightarrow 5 \times 13 = 65\) & \(63\) jigsaws.

Models both classical four-piece dissections of the \(8 \times 8 = 64\) square (two \(3 \times 8\) right triangles of area 12 + two \(5 \times (3, 5)\) right trapezoids of area 20) inside a single exact geometor.model.Model:

  1. Lower figure (:math:`5 times 13 = 65` Hooper/Loyd rectangle): encloses the slender internal parallelogram O P1 Q P2 of exact shoelace area +1;

  2. Upper figure (Sam Loyd Jr. / Dudeney / Knott 63-unit notched block, shifted to ``y >= 7``): two \(5 \times 6 = 30\) wings joined by a \(3 \times 1 = 3\) bridge (apparent outer area 63), concealing an internal overlap parallelogram U0 U1 U2 U3 of exact shoelace area +1 (\(64 - 1 = 63\)).

Returns:

Verified GeometricCatalogResult for KNOTT-6.2.

geometor.knott.identities.construct_hayashi_arctan_grid_model() → geometor.knott.flat_geometry.GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-6.4: Ko Hayashi’s integer-grid proof of \(\frac{\pi}{4} = \arctan\frac{1}{2} + \arctan\frac{1}{5} + \arctan\frac{1}{8}\).

Constructs four integer lattice points O(0,0), E(10,10), M(15,5), and N(16,2) (with horizontal reference X(16,0)):

  • Right triangle O E M has \(s([OE], [EM]) = 1\) and \(Q(E,M)/Q(O,E) = 50/200 = 1/4 = (1/2)^2\), proving \(\angle EOM = \arctan(1/2)\) between \(45^\circ\) ray OE and slope-\(1/3\) ray OM;

  • Right triangle O M N has \(s([OM], [MN]) = 1\) and \(Q(M,N)/Q(O,M) = 10/250 = 1/25 = (1/5)^2\), proving \(\angle MON = \arctan(1/5)\) between slope-\(1/3\) ray OM and slope-\(1/8\) ray ON.

Returns:

Verified GeometricCatalogResult for KNOTT-6.4.

Construct KNOTT-6.5: Cylindrical Golden-Angle parastichy lattice & opposing Fibonacci spirals.

Unrolls cylindrical phyllotaxis onto the \((\Delta x, n)\) plane around stem axis [O S] (\(x = 0\)), plotting exact Fibonacci primordia \(P_k = (20 (F_k \phi^2 - F_{k-2}),\; F_k) = (20 (-\phi)^k,\; F_k)\) for \(k = 2..6\) (\(F_2=1, F_3=2, F_4=3, F_5=5, F_6=8\)), plus their vector sum completing fundamental parastichy parallelograms O P3 Q34 P4 and O P4 Q45 P5.

Verified GeometricCatalogResult for KNOTT-6.5.
