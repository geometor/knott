geometor.knott.trigonometry¶

Collection 2: Exact Pentagonal & Constructible Trigonometry (KNOTT-2.1 – KNOTT-2.2).

Catalogs closed-form radical sines, cosines, tangents, and Rational Trigonometry spreads for the pentagonal family (\(18^\circ, 36^\circ, 54^\circ, 72^\circ\)), the complete 29-angle \(3^\circ k\) constructible lattice (\(k = 1\dots 29\)), and Gauss’s regular 17-gon cosine curated by Dr. Ron Knott on simpleTrig.html and phi2DGeomTrig.html.

geometor.knott.trigonometry.pentagonal_trig_table() → dict[int, ExactAngleEntry]¶

Return exact radical trigonometric entries for \(18^\circ, 36^\circ, 54^\circ, 72^\circ\).

Verifies every entry against \(\sin^2 + \cos^2 = 1\), \(\cos 36^\circ - \sin 18^\circ = 1/2\), \(\cos 36^\circ \cdot \sin 18^\circ = 1/4\), and Wildberger’s 5-fold spread polynomial \(S_5(s) = s(16 s^2 - 20 s + 5)^2 = 0\) for \(36^\circ\) and \(72^\circ\).

Returns:

Mapping {18: ..., 36: ..., 54: ..., 72: ...} of verified ExactAngleEntry records.

geometor.knott.trigonometry.construct_pentagonal_trig_model() → geometor.knott.flat_geometry.GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-2.1: First-quadrant unit circle pentagonal rays & golden projections.

Places rays at \(18^\circ, 36^\circ, 54^\circ, 72^\circ\) on unit circle (O, U) and drops perpendiculars C36 (\(x = \Phi/2\)) and C72 (\(x = \phi/2\)) onto [O U], demonstrating \(OC_{36} - OC_{72} = 1/2\) and \(OC_{36} \cdot OC_{72} = 1/4\).

Returns:

Verified GeometricCatalogResult for KNOTT-2.1.

geometor.knott.trigonometry.three_degree_lattice() → dict[int, ExactAngleEntry]¶

Compute closed-form radicals and spreads for all 29 angles \(3^\circ k\) (\(k = 1\dots 29\)).

Decomposes every integer multiple of \(3^\circ\) in \((0^\circ, 90^\circ)\) as a single sum or difference \(a \cdot 18^\circ \pm b \cdot 15^\circ\) between the pentagonal subfield \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5}, \sqrt{10 \pm 2\sqrt{5}})\) and the hexagonal/octagonal subfield \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3})\).

Returns:

Ordered mapping {3: ..., 6: ..., ..., 87: ...} of 29 verified ExactAngleEntry records.

geometor.knott.trigonometry.gauss_seventeen_gon_cosine() → sympy.Expr¶

Return Gauss’s exact nested quadratic radical for \(\cos(\pi / 17)\) (1796).

Cataloged by Dr. Ron Knott on simpleTrig.html#pi17 as the Fermat-prime bridge beyond Euclid’s \(2^a \cdot 3 \cdot 5\) angle lattice. Verified algebraically via Chebyshev’s polynomial \(T_{17}(\cos(\pi / 17)) + 1 = 0\) reduced step-by-step in Gauss’s quadratic field tower \(\mathbb{Q} \subset K_1 \subset K_2 \subset K_3 \subset K_4\).

Returns:

Exact radical expression for \(\cos(\pi / 17)\).

geometor.knott.trigonometry.construct_three_degree_lattice_model() → geometor.knott.flat_geometry.GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-2.2: Euclid IV.16 regular 15-gon superposition & \(3^\circ\) lattice.

Superimposes equilateral-triangle ray P60 (\(60^\circ\), \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})\)) and pentagonal central ray P72 (\(72^\circ\), \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})\)) on unit circle (O, U) to isolate the \(12^\circ\) regular 15-gon arc P60 P72 (and origin ray P12), together with bisected rays P6 (\(6^\circ\)) and P3 (\(3^\circ\)), and Ailles’ \(15^\circ\) ray P15 (\(18^\circ - 15^\circ = 3^\circ\)).

Returns:

Verified GeometricCatalogResult for KNOTT-2.2.
