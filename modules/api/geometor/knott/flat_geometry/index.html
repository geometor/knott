geometor.knott.flat_geometry¶

Catalogs the planar compass-and-straightedge constructions, self-similar triangles, pentagonal star-chord progressions, Kepler right triangles, and Penrose aperiodic prototiles curated by Dr. Ron Knott on phi2DGeomTrig.html.

geometor.knott.flat_geometry.construct_golden_section_segment() → GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-1.1: Internal and external golden sections on segment AB.

Models both classical Euclidean constructions on segment AB of length 2:

  1. External extension: From square ABCD and midpoint M of AB, circle (M, C) of radius \(\sqrt{5}\) meets line [AB] at E, yielding \(AE / AB = AB / BE = \Phi\) and Golden Rectangle AEFD.

  2. Internal division: Joining A to midpoint H of BC, circle (H, B) cuts AH at K (\(AK = 2\phi\)), and circle (A, K) cuts AB at the internal golden section G (\(AB / AG = AG / GB = \Phi\)).

Construct KNOTT-1.2: Golden rectangle, \(\sqrt{5}\) rectangle, & whirling squares.

Surrounding unit square ABCD with midpoint circle (M, C) constructs both right golden rectangle AEFD (\(\Phi \times 1\)) and full \(\sqrt{5} \times 1\) rectangle WEFN. Subdividing gnomon BEFC into whirling squares demonstrates that main diagonal [AF] and gnomon diagonal [EC] intersect orthogonally (exact spread \(s = 1\)) at the logarithmic spiral pole P.

Construct KNOTT-1.3: Acute golden triangle (\(72^\circ\text{-}72^\circ\text{-}36^\circ\)) & gnomon deflation.

Constructs acute golden triangle ABC (\(AB = 1, AC = BC = \Phi\)) and intersects leg BC with unit circle (A, B) at D, partitioning ABC into obtuse golden gnomon CAD (\(36^\circ\text{-}36^\circ\text{-}108^\circ\), legs 1) and deflated acute golden triangle DAB (\(AB = AD = 1, BD = \phi\)), followed by a second deflation step at E on AB.

Construct KNOTT-1.4: Regular pentagon, pentagram \(1:\Phi:\Phi^2:\Phi^3\) chords, & paper knot.

Constructs regular pentagon P0 P1 P2 P3 P4 of unit side, intersects its five pentagram diagonals to form inner pentagon Q0 Q1 Q2 Q3 Q4, verifies the four-step golden geometric progression along P0 P2, and drops perpendicular P4 H onto [P0 P1] to prove Dr. Ron Knott’s overhand paper-strip knot theorem: two crossing parallel-sided strips of width \(w = P_4 H = \sin 72^\circ\) lock into an exact regular pentagon of side \(w \sec 18^\circ\).

geometor.knott.flat_geometry.construct_kepler_triangle() → GeometricCatalogResult¶

Construct KNOTT-1.5: Kepler’s golden right triangle \((1, \sqrt{\Phi}, \Phi)\) & near-quadrature.

Uses Euclid’s semicircle geometric-mean construction on DE (\(D=(-1,0), B=(0,0), E=(\Phi,0)\)) to construct \(C=(0, \sqrt{\Phi})\) by exact compass intersection over \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\Phi})\). In right triangle DBC:

  • Side quadrances \((Q(B,D), Q(B,C), Q(D,C)) = (1, \Phi, \Phi^2)\) form a geometric progression AND satisfy Pythagoras \(1 + \Phi = \Phi^2\);

  • The square BCFG on leg BC has perimeter \(4\sqrt{\Phi} \approx 5.08808\), while the Thales circle (M, C) on hypotenuse CD has circumference \(\pi\Phi \approx 5.08320\) (agreement within \(0.096\%\)).

Construct KNOTT-1.6: Penrose kites & darts (\(P2\)) and thick/thin golden rhombs (\(P3\)).

Constructs thick Penrose golden rhomb ABCD (\(72^\circ / 108^\circ\), side \(\Phi\)), intersects diagonal AC with unit circle (A, U) at K to partition ABCD into Penrose dart ABKD (non-convex \(216^\circ\) reflex at K) and Penrose kite KBCD (\(72^\circ, 72^\circ, 72^\circ, 144^\circ\)), and adjoins thin Penrose golden rhomb ABEF (\(36^\circ / 144^\circ\), side \(\Phi\)). Proves both exact area ratios equal \(\Phi : 1\).
