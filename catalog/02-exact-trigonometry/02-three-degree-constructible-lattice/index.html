KNOTT-2.2 — The Complete 3° Constructible Angle Lattice (3°k for k = 1..29)¶

Superimposing pentagonal angles in Q(√5) with equilateral and half-right angles in Q(√2, √3) expresses sin(3°k), cos(3°k), tan(3°k), and spread s(3°k) in explicit nested square roots for all 29 acute multiples of 3°, while Gauss’s Fermat prime 17 = 2⁴ + 1 extends compass constructibility to cos(π/17).

Constructive Synthesis & Exact Proof¶

Combining the pentagonal subfield \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5}, \sqrt{10 \pm 2\sqrt{5}})\) with the equilateral and half-right subfield \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3})\) generates closed-form square-root radicals for all 29 acute multiples of \(3^\circ\) (KnottSimpleTrig).

  • Euclid IV.16 Regular 15-Gon & 3° Generator: On unit circle (O, U), superimposing equilateral ray OP60 (\(60^\circ\)) and pentagonal ray OP72 (\(72^\circ\)) isolates the \(12^\circ\) regular pentadecagon arc, making chord P60P72 and origin-based chord UP12 equal. Equivalently, subtracting Ailles’ \(15^\circ\) ray OP15 from pentagonal ray OP18 (or bisecting OP12 through OP6 to OP3) yields the atomic constructible angle \(3^\circ = 18^\circ - 15^\circ\), whose chord P15P18 and unit-base chord UP3 are equal.

  • Gauss’s Regular 17-Gon Bridge: While \(1^\circ\) cannot be constructed with compass and straightedge (requiring cube roots via angle trisection), Carl Friedrich Gauss (Gauss1801) proved that Fermat prime \(F_2 = 2^{2^2} + 1 = 17\) admits a four-step quadratic radical tower for \(\cos(\pi / 17)\), verified here symbolically via Chebyshev’s polynomial \(T_{17}(\cos(\pi / 17)) + 1 = 0\).
