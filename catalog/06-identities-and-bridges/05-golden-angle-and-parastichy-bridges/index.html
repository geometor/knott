KNOTT-6.5 — Golden Angle (360° / Φ² ≈ 137.50776°) & Parastichy Bridges¶

Dividing a full turn in extreme and mean ratio yields the Golden Angle 2π / Φ² = 2π φ² ≈ 137.50776°, whose cylindrical primordia satisfy Fₖ φ² − Fₖ₋₂ = (−φ)ᵏ, pairing opposing left- and right-handed parastichy spirals in consecutive Fibonacci numbers (Fₖ, Fₖ₊₁).

Constructive Synthesis & Exact Proof¶

Concluding the catalog on fibnat.html (KnottNature), Dr. Ron Knott connects the Golden Angle \(2\pi / \Phi^2 = 2\pi\phi^2 \approx 137.50776^\circ\) to the opposing Fibonacci spiral families (parastichies) studied by Luca Pacioli, D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson (Thompson1917), and H. S. M. Coxeter (Coxeter1973).

  • Unrolled Cylindrical Primordia & Alternating Convergence: Unrolling a cylindrical stem around central axis OS (O to S), the \(F_k\)-th primordium lands at signed angular offset \(F_k \phi^2 - F_{k-2} = (-\phi)^k\), placing P2 (\(F_2 = 1\)), F2 (\(F_3 = 2\)), F3 (\(F_4 = 3\)), F5 (\(F_5 = 5\)), and F8 (\(F_6 = 8\)) on alternating sides of OS along vectors OP2 – OF8.

  • Unimodular Parastichy Parallelograms: Because \((-\phi)^k + (-\phi)^{k+1} = (-\phi)^{k+2}\) and \(F_k + F_{k+1} = F_{k+2}\), vector F5 is the exact 2D vector sum of F2 and F3 (edges F2F5 and F3F5), and F8 is the vector sum of F3 and F5 (edges F3F8 and F5F8). Moreover, \(\phi^k (F_k \phi + F_{k+1}) = \phi^k \Phi^k = 1\), proving that consecutive parastichy cells OF3F5F2 and OF3F8F5 are equal unimodular fundamental domains of normalized area \(1\).
