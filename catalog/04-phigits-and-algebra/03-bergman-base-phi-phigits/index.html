KNOTT-4.3 — Bergman’s Base-Φ (‘Phigit’) Numeral System¶

Under the carry law Φⁿ = Φⁿ⁻¹ + Φⁿ⁻² (100_Φ = 011_Φ), every positive integer N ≥ 1 admits a unique finite minimal non-adjacent {0, 1} expansion in powers of Φ; excluding infinite 011-tails yields a unique 00-free maximal form and exactly Fₖ valid finite phigit forms for every Fibonacci number Fₖ.

Constructive Synthesis & Exact Proof¶

Discovered in 1957 by fourteen-year-old George Bergman (Bergman1957) and extensively developed by Dr. Ron Knott on phigits.html (KnottPhigits), the base-Φ (“phigit”) numeral system represents every positive integer uniquely as a finite non-adjacent binary sum of powers of the irrational radix \(\Phi\).

  • Carry Law & Minimal Phigit Scale: Along base-\(\Phi\) axis OP3 carrying ticks Pm2 (\(\Phi^{-2}\)), Pm1 (\(\Phi^{-1}\)), P0 (\(\Phi^0 = 1\)), P1 (\(\Phi^1\)), P2 (\(\Phi^2\)), and P3 (\(\Phi^3\)), the identity \(\Phi + \Phi^{-2} = 2\) decomposes \(2 = 10.01_\Phi\) into segments A2M2 and M2B2 (aligned to P1 via P1M2), \(3 = 100.01_\Phi\) into A3M3 and M3B3, and \(4 = 101.01_\Phi\) into A4M4 (framing OP2M4A4 via P2M4), M4N4, and N4B4.

  • Maximal Phigit Form & Fibonacci Multiplicity: Applying \(100_\Phi \to 011_\Phi\) (while forbidding non-terminating \(\dots 011\) tails via \(101_\Phi \to 01111_\Phi\)) produces a unique 00-free maximal phigit expansion, and every Fibonacci integer \(F_k\) admits exactly \(F_k\) valid finite phigit representations while Édouard Lucas numbers \(L_{2m}\) achieve local minima.
