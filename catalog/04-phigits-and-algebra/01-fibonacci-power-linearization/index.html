KNOTT-4.1 — Fibonacci Power Linearization (Φⁿ = FₙΦ + Fₙ₋₁) & The Phibonacci Brick¶

Every integer power n ∈ Z of the Golden Ratio reduces to the linear form Φⁿ = Fₙ Φ + Fₙ₋₁ and (−φ)ⁿ = Fₙ₊₁ − Fₙ Φ = Fₙ₋₁ − Fₙ φ, while a rectangular Phibonacci brick of edges (1, φ, Φ) has unit volume 1, surface area 4Φ, and integer space diagonal 2.

Constructive Synthesis & Exact Proof¶

Because \(\Phi^2 = \Phi + 1\), Dr. Ron Knott (KnottPropsPhi) emphasizes that \(\Phi\) is the unique positive proportion whose geometric power sequence \(1, \Phi, \Phi^2, \Phi^3, \dots\) is simultaneously an additive Fibonacci sequence (\(\Phi^{n+1} = \Phi^n + \Phi^{n-1}\)):

  • Linearization of All Integer Powers: Induction over \(n \in \mathbb{Z}\) reduces every positive and negative power to \(\Phi^n = F_n \Phi + F_{n-1}\) and \((-\phi)^n = F_{n+1} - F_n \Phi\). Visually, the \(\Phi^2 \times \Phi^2\) square AEFH on A, E, F, H (with main diagonal AF) decomposes via internal cuts BJ and DG through C into unit square ABCD (\(1\)), \(\Phi \times \Phi\) square CGFJ (\(\Phi^2\)), and two \(1 \times \Phi\) golden rectangles (\(\Phi^4 = 3\Phi + 2 = 1 + 2\Phi + \Phi^2\)).

  • The Unit-Volume Phibonacci Brick: Alongside the square, rectangle APQR (with edges PQ and RQ) displays the unfolded cross-section of Dr. Knott’s 3D Phibonacci brick of edges \((1, \phi, \Phi)\), whose volume is exact unit \(1 \cdot \phi \cdot \Phi = 1\), total surface area is \(2(\phi + \Phi + 1) = 4\Phi\), and 3D space diagonal has integer length \(\sqrt{1 + \phi^2 + \Phi^2} = \sqrt{4} = 2\).
