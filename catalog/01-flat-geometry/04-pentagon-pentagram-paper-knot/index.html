KNOTT-1.4 — Regular Pentagon, Pentagram, Decagon & Paper-Knot Pentagon¶

The five diagonals of a regular pentagon of side 1 enclose a similar inner pentagon of side φ² and cut each diagonal in the geometric progression φ² : φ : 1 : Φ; furthermore, folding a flat uniform strip of width w into a tight overhand knot locks parallel trapezoid edges into an exact regular pentagon of side w sec 18°.

Constructive Synthesis & Exact Proof¶

Every regular pentagon P0P1P2P3P4 of unit edge P0P1 \(= 1\) is locked by golden triangles (KnottPhi2D, Pacioli1509):

  • Pentagram Progression: All five star diagonals—P0P2, P0P3, P1P3, P1P4, and P2P4—are equal of exact length \(\Phi\). Their pairwise intersections Q0, Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4 bound a similar inverted inner pentagon Q0Q1Q2Q3Q4 of side \(\phi^2 = 2 - \Phi\), dividing each pentagram diagonal in the four-step geometric progression \((Q_1 Q_2,\; P_0 Q_1,\; P_0 Q_2,\; P_0 P_2) = (\phi^2,\; \phi,\; 1,\; \Phi)\).

  • Overhand Paper-Strip Knot: Dropping altitude P4H from vertex P4 onto the extended base line of P0P1 (parallel to opposite diagonal P2P4) at H isolates the parallel strip width \(w = \sin 72^\circ = \frac{1}{4}\sqrt{10 + 2\sqrt{5}}\). Consequently, folding a uniform paper ribbon of width \(w = 1\) into a flat overhand knot forces an exact regular pentagon of side quadrance \(Q_{\text{side}} = \sec^2 18^\circ = \frac{8}{5 + \sqrt{5}} = \frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{5} \Phi^2\).
